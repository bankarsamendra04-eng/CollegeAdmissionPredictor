About the Platform

Intelligent guidance powered by historical cutoff analytics and machine learning

Our Mission

Engineering college admissions across Maharashtra (MHT-CET / JEE Main) involve hundreds of colleges, multiple reservation categories, rounds of centralized admission (CAP), and shifting cutoff trends. Finding the best colleges that match a student's merit rank is often stressful and complex.

The AI College Admission Predictor aims to bring transparency and data-driven clarity to students and parents by evaluating multi-year cutoff statistics, branch preferences, institutional quality, and placement metrics to provide calibrated probability estimates and actionable recommendations.

How the Engine Works

1. Machine Learning Classification

Trained on historical student merit ranks, category quotas, branch codes, and closing trends using ensemble algorithms (Random Forest & Gradient Boosting) with probability calibration via CalibratedClassifierCV.

2. Rule-Based Fallback & Trend Analyzer

When specific ML feature sets are sparse, a deterministic rule-based engine compares rank ratios against historical cutoffs and adjusts for multi-year trend velocity (improving, stable, or easing cutoffs).

3. Multi-Factor Recommendation Scoring

Recommendations are ranked through a weighted multi-attribute utility function combining admission probability, branch affinity, institutional placement record, accreditation, and location match.

Categories & Classifications

SAFE (≥ 75%)

Strong advantage. Historical closing ranks are well above your rank.

MODERATE (40-74%)

Competitive chance. Admission possible in subsequent CAP rounds.

DREAM (15-39%)

Ambitious target. Favorable fluctuations in cutoffs required.

UNLIKELY (< 15%)

Highly competitive relative to merit rank.
