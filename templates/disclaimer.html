Key Factors Influencing Actual Admissions

  • Official Quotas and Reservations: Final seats depend on government reservation policies, defense quotas, PwD reservations, TFWS seats, and linguistic/religious minority statuses.
  • Applicant Volatility: Student preference trends fluctuate each year based on industry demand, placement news, and institutional reputations.
  • CAP Rounds & Vacancy Rounds: Spot rounds and institutional vacancy rounds have distinct eligibility rules.
  • Seat Matrix Updates: DTE/CET Cell and AICTE may revise seat counts or introduce/merge branches prior to CAP rounds.

Recommended Action

Always consult official cutoff booklets, circulars, and the official admission authority portals (e.g., State CET Cell Maharashtra, JoSAA, CSAB, DTE) and verify directly with the respective college admission offices.
